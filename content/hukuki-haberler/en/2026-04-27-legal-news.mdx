## Legal Profession News

### 2026 Bar Entrance Exam (HMGS/1)

The 2026 Bar Entrance Exam was held on Sunday, 26 April, across 17 provinces at 18 examination centres in 36 buildings and 462 halls. A total of **12,993 candidates** sat the exam. The question booklet and answer key have been published by ÖSYM; results are expected on **21 May 2026**.

[Question booklet and answer key →](https://www.osym.gov.tr/TR,34003/2026-hmgs1-temel-soru-kitapcigi-ve-cevap-anahtari-yayimlandi-26042026.html)

### Ministry of Justice Launches AI-Assisted Legal Services Project

Justice Minister **Akın Gürlek** announced an AI-assisted legal services project at the historic Peykler Madrasa in Edirne. Under the project, citizens will be able to describe their disputes — covering rent, alimony and debt — and receive automatically generated **petition templates**, guidance on likely outcomes, and evidence recommendations.

**All 78 Turkish bar associations** responded with a joint statement, calling for careful evaluation of the project's impact on the right to a fair trial, the right to defence, and legal certainty.

[Turkish Bar Association joint statement →](https://www.barobirlik.org.tr/Haberler/adalete-gercek-anlamda-erisim-icin-oncelik-savunmanin-guclendirilmesi-olmalidir-86436)

A biometric monitoring system developed by the Ministry of Justice and TÜRKSAT has reached the tender stage. Once implemented:

## Supreme Court Rulings

### Criticism of Public Figures — Broader Tolerance Standard

**Court of Cassation, 4th Criminal Chamber** (File No. E. 2023/16112, K. 2026/296, 06.01.2026):

> Politicians, journalists and other public figures must tolerate a wider margin of criticism compared to private individuals.

---

### Poverty Alimony — Woman Earning Minimum Wage

**Court of Cassation, 2nd Civil Chamber** (File No. E. 2025/2575, K. 2025/9513, 05.11.2025):

> Where the husband earns above the minimum wage and the wife earns at minimum-wage level, the wife's income does not lift her out of poverty; accordingly, **poverty alimony** must be awarded in her favour.

---

### Family Residence Declaration Does Not Invalidate Prior Vacation Pledge

**Court of Cassation, 3rd Civil Chamber** (File No. E. 2025/3374, K. 2025/4997, 20.10.2025):

> A subsequent family residence declaration cannot retroactively invalidate a **vacation pledge** that the tenant had previously given and which was legally effective at the time it was made.

---

### Evidence Obtained Without a Written Search Warrant Is Inadmissible

**Court of Cassation, 7th Criminal Chamber** (File No. E. 2021/5762, K. 2025/8147, 27.05.2025):

> Materials obtained without a written search warrant or a judicial search order, as well as evidence gathered by police officers acting undercover without official undercover-agent status, **may not form the basis of a conviction**.

## Legislation

### Specialist Courts' Jurisdiction Reorganised

By decision of the **Supreme Council of Judges and Prosecutors (HSK) First Chamber**, specific subject-matter jurisdictions have been allocated to Ankara administrative courts:

**Ankara Administrative Courts No. 2, 3, 4 and 16** will hear cases arising under:
- Law No. 3194 (**Zoning Law**)
- Law No. 6306 (**Urban Transformation**)
- Law No. 3621 (**Coastal Law**)
- Law No. 2863 (**Cultural Heritage Protection**)
- Law No. 4708 (**Building Inspection**)

**Ankara Administrative Courts No. 10, 13 and 25**: Cases involving the Capital Markets Board (SPK), Competition Authority and Public Oversight Authority (KGK).

**Ankara Administrative Courts No. 12, 14 and 15**: Cases involving RTÜK, BTK, EPDK, NDK and KVKK (Personal Data Protection Authority).

### Parental Leave Extended — Social Media Age Restriction of 15

A law adopted by the Grand National Assembly introduces:

- **Maternity leave**: extended from 16 to **24 weeks**
- **Paternity leave**: extended from 5 to **10 days**
- A **ban on providing social media services to children under 15**
- Mandatory **age-verification measures** for social network providers
