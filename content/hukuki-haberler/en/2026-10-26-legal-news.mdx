Turkey's legal agenda for 26 October 2026 opens with the Istanbul Bar Association presidential election. Key **Turkish law updates** this week include new legislation protecting investment fund victims, the launch of Turkey's Emission Trading System regulatory framework, four fresh Court of Cassation rulings, and leadership changes at the Capital Markets Board and the Public Oversight Authority.

## Legal Profession & Judiciary

### Illegal Egg Trade Syndicate: 36 Arrested in 14 Provinces

Justice Minister Akın Gürlek announced a simultaneous operation across 14 provinces targeting an organisation involved in illegal reproductive cell (egg) trade. Of 53 suspects, 36 were detained; the group included 41 donor women, 2 gynaecologists and 4 travel agents. The operation is linked to networks operating in Northern Cyprus, Albania and Georgia.

**Legal context:** The trade in human tissue and reproductive cells is governed by **Law No. 2238 on the Taking, Storage and Transplantation of Organs and Tissues**. Article 15 expressly prohibits organ and tissue commerce; violations carry criminal sanctions under the Turkish Penal Code. The cross-border dimension triggers international legal cooperation procedures including extradition and rogatory requests.

### Staged Accident Fraud: 17 Insurers Victimised

Adana Public Prosecutor's Office detained 77 of 88 suspects after establishing that 76 traffic accidents between 2022 and 2026 were staged. Investigators found that damaged vehicles were reused with different drivers to fabricate accidents, and false accident reports were prepared.

**Legal framework:** Insurance fraud constitutes "aggravated fraud" under **Turkish Penal Code Article 158**. Where an organised criminal structure is identified — as here — **TPC Article 220** (establishing or membership in a criminal organisation) applies concurrently; penalties for both offences are imposed independently.

### Istanbul Bar Association Elects Kocasakal as President

At the 128. Ordinary General Assembly of the Istanbul Bar Association — involving approximately 70,000 voting members — **Prof. Dr. Ümit Kocasakal** was elected president, according to unofficial results, defeating eight other candidates. Kocasakal, a previous holder of the position, will serve for the **2026–2028** term. A dispute broke out between opposing groups on the first day of the assembly on 24 October; the presiding board called a 15-minute recess and prohibited flag use in campaign activities.

**Bar associations in Turkey:** Bars operate as public professional bodies under **Attorneys' Law No. 1136**. The Istanbul Bar president holds a vote in the Turkish Bar Association (TBB) general assembly, making this election relevant to national-level legal policy representation.

### HSK Mandates Active Caseloads for Senior Prosecutors

The **Council of Judges and Prosecutors (HSK)** issued rules requiring deputy chief public prosecutors and chief public prosecutors of courts of first instance to participate in judicial proceedings in addition to their administrative duties. Quota rates range from **15–25%** for deputy chiefs and **30–40%** for first-instance chiefs, depending on total prosecutor count. A one-month adaptation period was granted.

**Practical impact:** The reform aims to deploy experienced prosecutors' expertise more widely and distribute workloads more evenly. Parties with pending cases may wish to monitor whether their prosecutor changes as a result. [Source →](https://www.hsk.gov.tr/cumhuriyet-bassavci-vekilleri-ile-asliye-ceza-mahkemeleri-nezdindeki-cumhuriyet-bassavcilarinin-adl_4422)

## Official Gazette & Parliament

### SPK and KGK Heads Removed by Presidential Decree

By presidential decree, **Capital Markets Board (SPK) Chairman Mahmut Sütcü** and **Public Oversight, Accounting and Auditing Standards Authority (KGK) Chairman İbrahim Ömer Gönül** were removed from office. [Source →](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/10/20261020-8.pdf)

**Legal status:** The SPK operates as an independent regulatory authority under **Capital Markets Law No. 6362**; its chair is appointed and dismissed by the President. The KGK was established by **Decree-Law No. 660** and holds authority to set accounting and auditing standards. Ongoing regulatory processes at both bodies may be affected by this transition.

### Three Districts Reassigned to Keşan Criminal Jurisdiction

The HSK General Assembly transferred **Malkara** (Tekirdağ) and **Gelibolu** and **Eceabat** (Çanakkale) districts from their current heavy criminal court jurisdictions to the **Keşan Heavy Criminal Court** jurisdiction, citing geographic accessibility.

**Practical impact:** This affects the competent court for pending and future heavy criminal cases in these districts. Whether ongoing proceedings transfer to Keşan should be assessed under **CCP Articles 4 and 18**.

### Eight Electricity Market Regulations Amended

A package amending eight electricity market regulations was published in the **Official Gazette** (Resmî Gazete). Key changes: consumers may install storage facilities at the same metering point; settlement moved from "hourly" to "settlement period" basis; distribution companies gain remote-control authority over unlicensed generation assets in emergencies. Parties that benefited from the **YEKDEM** (Renewable Energy Support Mechanism) using fraudulent documentation will have payments reclaimed with interest. [Source →](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/19.10.2026)

### Turkey's Emission Trading System Regulation Published

The Regulation on the Operation of the **Turkish Emission Trading System (ETS)** Market was published in the Official Gazette, establishing the rules for primary and secondary trading of emission allowances. **EPİAŞ** (Energy Markets Operating Company) was designated as market operator. [Source →](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/10/20261021-4.pdf)

**Legal context:** The ETS regulation forms part of Turkey's alignment with **EU Emission Trading Directive 2003/87/EC** and complements the domestic **Climate Law**. Manufacturing, energy and transport facilities subject to carbon emission caps will be directly affected; non-compliance triggers administrative fines.

### KGK Delays Non-Climate Sustainability Disclosure to 2027

The KGK decided to defer the comparative disclosure obligation for non-climate risks under **Turkish Sustainability Reporting Standard (TSRS 1)** to **2027** and to extend transition exemptions to the 20th reporting period.

**Who is affected:** Public interest entities — banks, listed companies, state economic enterprises — and large-scale undertakings within KGK's scope. The extension provides additional preparation time, but compliance infrastructure for 2027 obligations should not be deprioritised.

### Investment Fund Victims Entitled to 1M TL Advance Payment

The Grand National Assembly enacted legislation covering investment funds whose liquidation was ordered by the SPK on **17 September 2026**. Investors whose net contribution is below **1 million TL** are entitled to full recovery as an interim payment; those above that threshold receive up to 1 million TL as an advance.

**Key provisions:** Losses are primarily recovered from portfolio management companies; if that fails, the **SDIF (TMSF)** pursues recovery from persons identified by the SPK under public receivables collection rules. TMSF may be appointed receiver over responsible parties' assets and may sell them without waiting for the court outcome. Investors found to have engaged in market-manipulative conduct receive no payment.

**Practical impact:** This law directly affects tens of thousands of investors. Small claimants can receive partial payment before liquidation concludes; high-value claims remain subject to ongoing proceedings.

## Constitutional Court & Court Rulings

### Constitutional Court: YENİ Party Name Challenge Rejected

The Constitutional Court (AYM) General Assembly rejected, by majority, the Chief Public Prosecutor of the Court of Cassation's request to invalidate the name and abbreviation of the **YENİ Party**, following a petition by the Yenilik Party over similarity.

**Legal framework:** The name-similarity prohibition under **Political Parties Law No. 2820, Article 96** is construed narrowly: it requires confusion-causing similarity. The Court found that "YENİ" and "Yenilik" do not meet that threshold.

---

### AYM: Failure to Allow Questioning of Decisive Witnesses Violates Fair Trial

The AYM found a violation of the **right to a fair trial** under **Constitution Article 36** in a case where a conviction relied decisively on testimony from witnesses the defendant was never able to cross-examine at trial. [Source →](https://www.anayasa.gov.tr/tr/haberler/bireysel-basvuru-basin-duyurulari/anlatimlari-mahkumiyet-kararinda-belirleyici-olcude-delil-olarak-kullanilan-taniklarin-durusmada-sorgulanmalarina-firsat-verilmemesi-nedeniyle-yapilan-basvuruya-iliskin-karar)

**Legal context:** The ruling aligns with the **ECtHR's Al-Khawaja and Tahery v. United Kingdom** doctrine: where a conviction is based solely or decisively on written statements, the accused's inability to cross-examine the witness violates fair trial guarantees. This AYM decision provides a template for similar individual applications.

---

### AYM: Permanent-Contract Spouse Condition for Relocation Not Unconstitutional

The AYM ruled, by majority, that the requirement in **Decree-Law No. 663, Article 45/A** — that a contracted health professional may only request relocation on family unity grounds if their spouse holds a **permanent-post position** at another public institution — does not violate the Constitution.

**Practical impact:** This ruling will affect pending administrative court cases brought by contracted health workers seeking relocation. The condition remains in force; the AYM has now set a precedent closing off constitutional challenge on this specific ground.

### Consequential Damages Must Be Calculated Using the Basket Method

**Court of Cassation, 6th Civil Division, E. 2025/2406, K. 2026/2915, 01.07.2026**

A contractor's consequential loss arising from delayed collection of the outstanding contract price must be determined and awarded using the **basket method** (sepet hesabı) — the approach best suited to reflecting the currency's actual depreciation.

**Legal context:** Consequential damages (**Code of Obligations Article 122**) cover additional losses beyond the principal claim caused by the creditor's delay. Earlier case law often relied on fixed interest or CPI adjustments; this ruling mandates a composite index combining CPI, exchange rate and gold as benchmarks. In construction and contracting disputes the difference in quantum can be substantial.

---

### Irregular PTT Service: Liability Falls on PTT, Not Ministry of Justice

**Court of Cassation, 3rd Civil Division, E. 2025/4428, K. 2026/3342, 01.06.2026**

Where the PTT (Turkish postal service) irregularly served an auction notice, and the enforcement officer had no fault, liability for the loss arising from the auction's annulment rests with **PTT**, not the Ministry of Justice.

**Practical impact:** This clarifies a recurring jurisdictional dispute in damages claims over defective auction service. Fault lies with the service agent (PTT's employee) and is attributed to PTT; claims brought against the Treasury on these facts can be dismissed.

---

### Invoice Is Evidence of Debt If Not Contested Within Eight Days — Commercial Code Article 21

**Samsun Regional Court of Appeal, 3rd Civil Division, E. 2026/1006, K. 2026/1422, 21.09.2026**

Where the contractual relationship is proved and no objection is raised within **eight days** of service, an invoice constitutes evidence of the debt (**Commercial Code Article 21**). If both parties' books record the invoice and delivery of goods is thereby established, the burden of proving payment shifts to the defendant.

**Practical impact:** This ruling directly governs the distribution of the burden of proof in commercial disputes. Sellers should ensure proper invoice service; buyers must track the eight-day objection period with care.

### Tenancy Rights Transfer Automatically in a Partial Spin-Off

**Court of Cassation, 12th Civil Division, E. 2026/3829, K. 2026/4984, 01.07.2026**

In a partial spin-off, tenancy rights pass **automatically** to the acquiring company; rental payments made by the acquiring company are valid.

**Legal context:** Under **Commercial Code Article 168**, assets and liabilities attached to the spun-off portion transfer to the acquirer by universal succession. This ruling establishes that a landlord's consent is not required for the tenancy to continue; the lease simply follows the spun-off assets. A significant clarification for real estate investors and tenant companies undergoing restructuring.
