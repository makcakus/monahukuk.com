TTK Art. 894-901 create a special layer on top of general goods-carriage rules for what is colloquially known in Turkey as "evden eve nakliyat" — home or office relocation. The consistent theme: the mover's burden goes up, and the consumer's evidentiary and procedural burden goes down.

## Expanded Mover Duties

Under Art. 894, the general Book Four rules apply by default unless this special part or an applicable international convention says otherwise. Art. 895 flips the usual loading allocation: disassembling and reassembling furniture, and loading and unloading generally, become the mover's job automatically — and where the customer is a consumer, packaging and labeling shift to the mover too. Art. 896 drops the consignment-note requirement, simplifies the dangerous-goods notice a consumer must give, and shifts the burden of explaining customs requirements onto the mover.

## Cubic Meters, Not Kilograms

Because household goods are typically bulky rather than heavy, Art. 897 and Art. 899 replace the ordinary weight-based liability figures with a volume-based one: both the consumer's liability for damage caused to the mover, and the mover's liability cap for loss or damage to the goods, are calculated at 1,500 SDR per cubic meter of required loading space rather than per kilogram.

## Exemptions and Notice

Art. 898 lists exemption grounds tailored to relocation — valuables and jewelry, inadequate packaging where packaging remained the customer's job, live animals or plants, and goods the customer insisted on loading against the mover's warning — under the same rebuttable-presumption structure used in general carriage law. Art. 900 shortens the notice window sharply: three business days for visible damage (rather than immediate notice at delivery) and fourteen days for hidden damage (rather than seven).

## The Consumer Information Trap

Art. 901 is the enforcement mechanism behind all of this: if the mover fails to inform a consumer-sender in writing, clearly and legibly, about liability terms, insurance options, and the shortened notice rules before or at delivery, the mover loses every one of these privileges — the exemption grounds, the volume-based cap, and the shortened notice deadlines all disappear.

## Practical Takeaway

A foreign customer arranging a Turkish household relocation should ask for the mover's written liability and insurance disclosure up front — without it, the mover cannot rely on the reduced liability cap even if a dispute later arises.
