Foreign nationals holding a [family residence permit](/en/articles/family-residence-permit-spouse-turkish-citizen) through marriage to a Turkish citizen, or to a foreigner with a residence permit, often ask the same question once divorce proceedings begin: does the permit end together with the marriage? The answer is less severe than most expect. Article 34 of Law No. 6458 on Foreigners and International Protection (YUKK) addresses divorce specifically, allowing the foreign spouse to remain in Türkiye under defined conditions. This article covers the fate of the residence permit after divorce, the conditions for transferring to a short-term permit, and the exceptions that apply.

## Divorce Does Not Automatically Cancel the Family Permit

The common misconception is that a finalised divorce automatically cancels the family residence permit. In fact, Article 36 of YUKK lists the grounds for refusal, cancellation, or non-renewal separately, and identifies as a cancellation ground the failure to obtain a short-term permit after the conditions for the family permit have ceased to exist. It is not the divorce itself but the combination of losing eligibility **and** being unable to obtain a short-term permit that triggers cancellation. In practice, the existing family permit generally remains valid through its term, and the critical juncture is the **renewal request** — divorce is, in most cases, a ground for refusing renewal rather than an automatic cancellation trigger.

## Transferring to a Short-Term Permit: The 3-Year Rule

Paragraph six of Article 34 provides that, upon divorce, a foreigner who was married to a Turkish citizen may be granted a short-term residence permit **provided they have resided in Türkiye for at least three years under a family residence permit**. This transfer right is the core safeguard allowing the foreigner to continue their life in Türkiye once the marriage has ended. Only time actually spent under a family permit counts toward the three years; periods under other permit types are excluded.

Once the condition is met, the foreigner may apply to transfer, without prejudice to the right to apply for any other permit type they qualify for — one linked to a work permit or property ownership, for example.

## What If the Three-Year Condition Is Not Met?

If the foreigner has not yet completed three years under a family permit at the time of divorce, the transfer right does not, as a rule, arise, and the existing permit will not be renewed, or will be cancelled once the loss of eligibility is established. Whether the foreigner meets the conditions for another permit type (student, work, humanitarian) must then be separately assessed. A foreigner who qualifies for neither may face having to leave Türkiye, which makes early case management essential.

## The Domestic Violence Exception

The three-year rule is not applied rigidly to every divorce. Under the same paragraph, where a **court decision** establishes that the foreign spouse was a victim of domestic violence, a short-term permit may be granted **without the three-year requirement**. This exception prevents spouses who divorce because of domestic violence, and have not yet completed three years, from suffering compounded hardship due to the time shortfall alone. The decision — arising from criminal proceedings or protective measures — must be submitted to the Provincial Directorate of Migration Management.

## No Time Requirement If the Sponsoring Spouse Dies

Separately from divorce, the sponsor's death (the Turkish citizen or permit-holding foreigner) also raises the question of the family permit's fate. Paragraph seven of Article 34 allows a short-term permit to be granted to the dependent foreigner **without any time requirement**. The existing family permit remains valid until the end of its term, after which any new request is assessed under the general rules.

## If Divorce Proceedings Are Not Yet Finalised

The marital union legally continues until the divorce decree becomes final, so the family permit generally remains valid throughout that period. The Migration Authority may nonetheless examine, through interviews or address checks, whether the union has in fact broken down, and take into account, at renewal, separate residence or the grounds cited in the case. It is important to track the permit's expiry while proceedings are ongoing and file the appropriate application before it lapses.

## Children Under Custody

The custody arrangement determines which parent a child's residence permit continues to be linked to. If the custodial parent holds sponsor status, the child's family permit can continue linked to them; if not, the child's residence status must be separately assessed and a transfer to another permit type planned if necessary. A clearly settled custody arrangement in the divorce agreement or judgment speeds up processing before the Migration Authority.

## Practical Steps

- Notify the Provincial Directorate of Migration Management of the change in address and marital status once the decree becomes final.
- Calculate, from the permit records, whether the three-year period has been completed.
- Prepare the documents needed for transfer (final decree, address document, health insurance, proof of means).
- Where relying on the domestic violence exception, obtain the original or certified copy of the court decision.
- Where children are under custody, separately assess the custody decision and their residence status.
- Complete the application before the existing family permit expires.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my permit cancelled the moment divorce proceedings are filed?

No. The family permit generally remains valid until the decree becomes final. Cancellation only follows once eligibility has actually ceased and a renewal or transfer application has also failed.

### I divorced before completing three years — do I have any options?

If you can prove through a court decision that you were a victim of domestic violence, the three-year requirement does not apply. Outside that exception, assess whether you meet the conditions for another permit type (work, student, property-based).

### What happens to my permit if my former spouse passes away?

A short-term permit may be granted without any time requirement. Your existing family permit remains valid until the end of its term, after which your new application is assessed under the general rules.

### What if I don't have custody — what happens to my child's permit?

It depends on the custodial parent's sponsor status. If they lack it, the child's residence status must be assessed separately on its specific facts; seeking legal advice early is advisable.
