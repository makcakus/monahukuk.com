You settled in mediation, the other side undertook to pay — and the payment never came. The good news at this point: in most cases the document in your hands has the same force as a court judgment, and you do not need to sue again. You can go straight to the enforcement office.

But "in most cases" matters. Whether the document acquires that force depends on who signed it and on the subject matter of the dispute.

## Two Separate Routes: With and Without an Annotation

The Law provides two independent routes for a settlement document to qualify as an **instrument in the nature of a court judgment**.

### Route 1: No Annotation Required — Qualified Signature

A settlement document signed jointly by the parties **and their lawyers** together with the mediator qualifies as an instrument in the nature of a court judgment without any enforceability annotation. In commercial disputes the parties' personal signatures are not required; the signatures of **the lawyers and the mediator** suffice.

The single condition for this route is that **both parties are represented by a lawyer**. If one party has no lawyer, this route is closed.

The 3rd Civil Chamber of the Turkish Court of Cassation has expressly confirmed this: settlement documents within this scope are instruments in the nature of a court judgment without an enforceability annotation, the annotation is **optional**, and where the obligations recorded in the minutes are not performed the document can be enforced directly.

### Route 2: The Enforceability Annotation

The annotation is obtained from the civil court of peace, and granting it is a matter of **non-contentious jurisdiction**; as a rule the examination is conducted on the file. In family law disputes capable of mediation, however, the examination is conducted with a hearing.

A settlement carrying the annotation qualifies as an instrument in the nature of a court judgment.

## Which Court Grants the Annotation?

The competent court varies according to the subject matter of the dispute and the timing of the mediation:

- **Where mediation was used before an action was brought:** the civil court of peace of the place where the mediator practises.
- **Where mediation was used during an action:** the court hearing the action.
- **For settlement documents concerning immovables (Articles 17/B and 18/B):** the civil court of peace of the place where the immovable is situated. The 5th Civil Chamber applies this rule strictly and upholds decisions declining jurisdiction where the application was made to another court.
- **For international settlement agreements under the Singapore Convention (Article 17/A):** the **commercial court of first instance**; jurisdiction is determined in stages — the place agreed by the parties, the other party's domicile in Türkiye, its place of residence, and finally one of the courts of Ankara, Istanbul or Izmir.

## Cases Where the Annotation Is Mandatory

The annotation is not always optional. It is **mandatory** in the following disputes:

- Disputes concerning the transfer of an immovable or the creation of a limited right in rem over it (Article 17/B/4),
- Lease, partition of movables and immovables and dissolution of joint ownership, condominium and neighbour-law disputes (Article 18/B/3),
- International settlement agreements under the Singapore Convention (Article 17/A/1).

## What Does the Court Examine?

The scope of the annotation review is narrow: it is limited to **whether the content of the settlement is capable of mediation and of enforcement**. The court does not review whether the settlement is fair, whether the consideration is appropriate, or whether a party was misled.

For settlement documents concerning immovables, however, the review widens: the court also examines whether the **restrictions, procedures and principles** laid down in the laws in relation to immovables have been observed, may request information and documents from institutions and bodies, and may hold a hearing where necessary.

The request for an annotation is refused in two situations:

**If the settlement is not capable of mediation.** The 9th Civil Chamber held that minutes drawn up the day after the termination of an employment contract, containing a blanket release declaration that the employee waived all the listed claims, were incapable of mediation and of enforcement — taking into account the mandatory provision of Article 420/2 of the Turkish Code of Obligations — and ruled that no annotation could be granted.

**If the obligation is not capable of enforcement.** The 12th Civil Chamber gave a striking example: if the mediation minutes merely record a promise by the parties and do not contain the constitutive ruling of a court, enforcement proceedings cannot be founded on that document to **compel the debtor to conclude a lease contract**; a constitutive power such as the formation of a contract does not lie with the enforcement office.

## Holding an Annotation Is Not Enough by Itself

This is the costliest misconception in practice. According to the decision of the 12th Civil Chamber resolving a conflict between regional courts of appeal, the fact that a document carries an annotation does **not mean it can be the subject of judgment-based enforcement in all circumstances**. For a judgment to be enforceable it must contain an **order to perform** — to give, to do or to refrain from doing; provisions that merely establish the existence of a legal relationship or create a new legal situation are not capable of enforcement.

In the case at hand the claim in the settlement document had been made conditional ("if the occupancy permit is not obtained within the stated period, the following monthly amount will be paid"). The Chamber held that, because the claim was made conditional and the document contained no order to perform, it could not be the subject of judgment-based enforcement despite carrying an enforceability annotation.

This case law is the most important practical lesson on drafting a settlement document: every obligation in the document must be drafted so as to produce a **directly enforceable** result — payment of money, delivery or eviction — and **without being made conditional**.

## Service and Cost of the Annotation Request

Even though the examination is conducted on the file, the request must be served on the other party. The 9th Civil Chamber treated the giving of a decision without the parties being properly constituted as a ground for reversal, under Article 27 of the Turkish Code of Civil Procedure on the right to be heard.

The cost falls on you. According to the decision of the 3rd Civil Chamber resolving a conflict of case law, since the annotation request is a matter of non-contentious jurisdiction there are no two opposing sides in dispute; therefore **no attorney's fee is awarded in favour of the applicant and the litigation costs are left with the applicant**.

## Court Fees and Stamp Duty

A **fixed fee** is charged for the annotation application and for any appeal against the decisions given on it. Moreover, if the parties wish to use the settlement document in another official transaction without obtaining an annotation, **stamp duty is also charged at a fixed rate**.

This is one of the most concrete financial advantages of mediation over litigation: in a high-value settlement, no proportional court fee and no proportional stamp duty arise.

## How Are Enforcement Proceedings Started?

Once the instrument in the nature of a court judgment is in hand, the process is the same as with a court judgment: **judgment-based enforcement proceedings** are commenced. This means the debtor cannot stop the proceedings by a simple objection. The debtor may only seek a stay of enforcement on the limited grounds listed in Article 33 of the Enforcement and Bankruptcy Law (payment, limitation, grant of time).

The original of the settlement document or a certified copy is attached to the request for enforcement; where an annotation has been obtained, the copy bearing the annotation is filed.

For settlements concerning the transfer of an immovable there is a facility introduced in 2024: once the enforceability annotation has been granted, either party may apply **directly to the land registry office for registration**, and the registration is completed **without a formal deed being drawn up**. There is also no need to bring an action for cancellation and registration of title.

## Points to Watch When Drafting the Document

Almost all problems at the enforcement stage arise from defects in drafting. The settlement document should contain:

- The identity and address details of the parties and, for legal persons, the representative's authority,
- **Each item of claim named separately** — general expressions such as "all claims" invite later argument about which claims were covered, and also extinguish the right to sue on the matters covered,
- The amount payable, the currency, the **due date or instalment dates**, and the default interest,
- Where an immovable is involved, the block, parcel, share ratio and transfer price,
- The account details for payment,
- Who is to bear the mediation fee,
- The signatures of the parties, their lawyers and the mediator.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can attachment be levied directly on the basis of the settlement document?

If the document has acquired the character of a judgment and **contains an order to perform**, judgment-based enforcement proceedings can be commenced; once they become final, attachment is levied and the debtor cannot stop them by a simple objection. By contrast, settlements that make a claim conditional or merely record a promise cannot be the subject of judgment-based enforcement, even with an annotation.

### Q: If the other side does not pay despite the settlement, do I have to sue again?

No. No action may be brought on the matters agreed; since the document has the character of a judgment, it is enforced directly. A fresh action arises only where the invalidity of the document is asserted.

### Q: How long does it take to obtain an enforceability annotation?

The annotation request is a matter of non-contentious jurisdiction and, as a rule, is examined on the file; in practice it is concluded quickly. For documents concerning immovables the process may take longer, since the court can request information from institutions and bodies and may hold a hearing.

### Q: What can I do if the annotation request is refused?

An appeal may be brought against a refusal; a fixed fee is charged for that application. If the refusal stems from the content of the settlement not being capable of enforcement, the parties may need to redraft the settlement in an enforceable form.

### Q: Can a mediation agreement made abroad be enforced in Türkiye?

Yes, for commercial and international settlement agreements within the scope of the Singapore Convention; for these documents the enforceability annotation must be obtained from the **commercial court of first instance**. The Convention excludes agreements relating to consumer, family, succession and employment law.
