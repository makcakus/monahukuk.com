For foreigners wishing to travel, work, or settle in Turkey, denial of visa application is a serious obstacle. Denials in visa applications based on urgent reasons such as holidays, business trips, family visits, education, or treatment mean time and financial loss for foreigner. In Turkish law, the **administrative judicial route** is open against visa denial; this guide examines how the process is run and steps to take against denial decisions.

## Legal Nature of Visa Denial

Visa denial issued by Turkish consulates or competent authorities at border gates:

- Is an **administrative act**,
- Can be reviewed in form and substance,
- Generally made within Turkish consular regulation and foreigners legislation framework,
- Open to judicial review.

Visa denial reason is typically not provided in writing; this complicates judicial defence but doesn't preclude review.

## Common Visa Denial Reasons

### 1. Document Deficiency

Failure to fully submit required documents in visa application is most common rejection reason. Standard documents like passport validity, biometric photo, bank statement, invitation letter, hotel reservation, round-trip ticket must be complete.

### 2. Insufficient Means Claim

Denial may be issued when foreigner's ability to support themselves throughout stay in Turkey is doubted. Insufficient balance or irregular movement in bank statement creates this impression.

### 3. Previous Visa Violations

Foreigner having previously entered Turkey and overstaid visa creates violation record and may be denial reason in new applications.

### 4. Existing Restriction Code

Foreigners barred from entering Turkey have **restriction code** in system. Restriction-coded foreigner's visa application is automatically denied.

### 5. Public Order / Security Reason

When foreigner's presence in Turkey is assessed as threatening public order, security, or health, denial decision is issued.

### 6. False Declaration or Document

When misleading declaration or false document made in application is detected, denial and additional sanctions for foreigner apply.

### 7. Previously Rejected Applications

Previously rejected applications can be evaluated negatively in new applications.

## Filing Cancellation Lawsuit

Against visa denial:

- Within period prescribed by administrative procedure rules,
- At **Administrative Court**,
- With **stay of execution** request,

cancellation lawsuit can be filed. Competent court is determined by consulate or administration that issued decision; for central administration, typically Ankara Administrative Court has jurisdiction.

## Legal Arguments in Lawsuit

Main arguments to advance in cancellation lawsuit:

### 1. Lack of Reasoning

It can be defended that denial decision doesn't rest on concrete reasoning; contains only abstract phrasing like "found unsuitable." Administrative acts must be **reasoned**.

### 2. Vague or Inconsistent Reasoning

Even if reasoning is given, can be defended it isn't supported by concrete evidence or conflicts with claimed facts.

### 3. Equal Treatment Principle Violation

If can be shown that other foreigners' applications in same conditions were accepted while this application was denied, **equal treatment violation** claim is strong argument.

### 4. Violation of Proportionality Principle

When visa denial creates measure disproportionate to applicant's favourable elements, proportionality principle may be violated.

### 5. Wrong Application of Restriction Code

If denial was given with restriction code but code is erroneous or shouldn't be valid anymore, this situation forms defence basis.

## Stay of Execution

When filing lawsuit, requesting **stay of execution** is important. If request is accepted:

- Consequences from denial decision are temporarily removed,
- Foreigner may gain opportunity for application process renewal or temporary entry.

## Foreigner Filing Lawsuit in Own Name

Foreigner can file cancellation lawsuit without being personally in Turkey:

- A Turkish lawyer is authorised by **apostilled notarised power of attorney**,
- Sworn Turkish translation of power of attorney is done,
- Transaction can also be done at Turkish consulate for foreigner signature.

## Lawsuit Duration and Outcomes

Cancellation lawsuit typically:

- Can take several months,
- Stay of execution decided in shorter time,
- Appeal and cassation phases follow main decision.

Positive lawsuit conclusion:

- Visa denial is cancelled,
- Foreigner can re-apply or in some cases decision toward direct visa issuance can result,
- Restriction code removal can also be requested.

## Removal of Restriction Codes

The reason behind visa denial is often a **restriction code**. Cancellation of denial decision through judicial route doesn't automatically remove the code; additionally:

- **Restriction code removal** request must be made,
- If request rejected, cancellation lawsuit can also be filed against that decision,
- Different codes have different removal mechanisms.

## Re-Application Strategy

In some cases, instead of or in parallel with cancellation lawsuit, **new application with deficiencies cured** is also a strategy. In this approach:

- Concrete responses prepared against previous denial reasons,
- Documents meticulously completed,
- Financial situation evidenced more strongly,
- Inviter's support in Turkey strengthened.

## Preventive Approach

Getting professional support before visa application significantly reduces denial risk. Typical services:

- Document list audit,
- Preparation of invitation and accompanying documents,
- Financial document analysis,
- Guidance on cleaning previous denial or violation records.

## European Council Judiciary

Visa denial can in some situations be taken before **ECHR** (European Court of Human Rights); particularly when violation exists in dimension of family unity right, education right, or health right. This route is long and technical process and can be applied after national judicial routes are exhausted.
