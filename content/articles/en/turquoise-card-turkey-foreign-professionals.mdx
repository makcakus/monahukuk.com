For highly qualified foreigners who intend to settle in Türkiye and build a lasting presence here, Law No. 6735 on International Labour Force provides a special status that goes well beyond an ordinary work permit: the Turquoise Card. Article 11 of the Law, together with the Turquoise Card Regulation issued under it, sets out who may apply, how applications are assessed, what the three-year transition period involves, and when the card becomes indefinite. This article walks through that framework, the application process, and the rights the card confers.

## What Is the Turquoise Card and Who Qualifies?

Under Article 11 of Law No. 6735, the Turquoise Card is granted to foreigners whose application is deemed suitable in light of their education, professional experience, and contribution to science and technology, and the impact of their activity or investment in Türkiye on the national economy and employment, following the recommendations of the International Labour Policy Advisory Board. Article 5 of the Regulation breaks this into five categories: highly qualified labour force (education, remuneration, knowledge and experience); highly qualified investor (investment or export level, employment to be created); a scientist or researcher whose work is internationally strategic; individuals with international success in culture, art, or sport; and those who contribute to Türkiye's international recognition. Applications are assessed under a points-based system set by the General Directorate (Regulation Art. 9); for the investor, scientist, and arts/sports categories, a certificate of eligibility from the relevant public authority may also feed into the score.

## The Application Process and Competent Authority

The General Directorate of International Labour Force at the Ministry of Labour and Social Security evaluates Turquoise Card applications. Foreigners already in Türkiye apply directly through the Ministry's electronic system; those abroad apply through the Turkish diplomatic mission in their country of citizenship or legal residence. Where necessary, the Ministry consults relevant public institutions during assessment (Regulation Art. 13). As a rule, the residence permit application for the holder's spouse and dependent children must be filed together with the Turquoise Card application itself (Regulation Art. 7); if filed from abroad without this application, it may be completed within ninety days of entry into Türkiye. Foreigners seeking a different, entrepreneurship-based status may instead consider the [entrepreneur residence permit](/en/articles/entrepreneur-residence-permit-turkey) route.

## The Transition Period: The First Three Years and the Indefinite Card

A Turquoise Card is issued with the first three years as a transition period from the date it is granted (Law Art. 11/2). During this period, the Ministry may request information and documents from the employer or the foreigner about the activity carried out. The application to remove the transition-period notation and render the card indefinite must be filed once one hundred eighty days remain before that period expires, and in any event before it expires (Regulation Art. 17); a late or missing application leaves the card invalid. If the assigned expert's final report, due within thirty days, is positive, the notation is removed; if negative, the General Directorate may extend the transition period or cancel the card.

## Rights Granted to Spouses and Dependants

Article 11(3) provides that the spouse and dependent children of a Turquoise Card holder are issued a document — in lieu of a residence permit — certifying their status as relatives of the holder, letting them reside lawfully in Türkiye without a separate application. Under Regulation Article 7, this requires documents evidencing the marital bond or custody relationship and dependent status. Where there is no impediment on national security or public order grounds and the transition-period notation has already been removed, the holder and family members may also acquire Turkish citizenship through the exceptional route under Article 12 of Law No. 5901, if proposed by the Ministry.

## Grounds for Cancellation and Restriction

A Turquoise Card can also be cancelled independent of the holder's request. Under Regulation Article 22, grounds include: failing to enter Türkiye within six months of the card's validity date, or remaining abroad continuously for over two years without force majeure; failing to renew a passport or equivalent document; working in violation of the Law or unregistered; not having worked continuously for at least one year; an application based on false or misleading information; failing to submit requested information during the transition period; and a notification that the person's employment is objectionable on public order, security, or health grounds. Article 15(1)(h) of the Law separately lists a Turquoise Card holder exceeding the Ministry-set period of stay abroad among the general grounds for cancelling a work permit. Administrative appeal and cancellation lawsuit remedies apply as with other work permit decisions; the procedure in our article on [appealing a work permit refusal](/en/articles/work-permit-denial-appeal-turkey) largely applies to Turquoise Card cancellations too.

## How It Differs From Other Work Permits

A Turquoise Card holder enjoys all rights conferred by the indefinite work permit under the Law (Law Art. 11/4, Regulation Art. 19): exemption from military service in Türkiye, and — unless a specific law requires otherwise — the same legislation applied to Turkish citizens governs residence, travel, employment, investment, commercial activity, inheritance, and acquisition or disposal of property. This sets the Turquoise Card apart from time-limited permits and from the independent work permit tied to a specific employer; it remains valid even where the holder changes employer. For foreigners who do not qualify for this special status but still want an indefinite right of residence, the general [long-term residence permit](/en/articles/long-term-residence-permit-turkey-requirements) requirements offer an alternative — though that permit does not confer a right to work, whereas the Turquoise Card directly includes one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many years does it take for a Turquoise Card to become indefinite?

A Turquoise Card is issued with the first three years as a transition period. Once one hundred eighty days remain before that period expires, an application filed before the deadline can have the transition-period notation removed, making the card indefinite; if the application is not filed in time, the card becomes invalid.

### Do my spouse and children need a separate residence permit?

No, as a rule they do not. The spouse and dependent children of a Turquoise Card holder are issued a document in lieu of a residence permit certifying their status as relatives of the cardholder; this application should, as a rule, be filed at the same time as the Turquoise Card application.

### What happens if my Turquoise Card is cancelled?

A cancellation decision renders the card invalid, and the work and residence rights attached to it come to an end. Administrative appeal and judicial review remain available against the decision, following a procedure broadly similar to other work permit cancellations.

### Which authority handles Turquoise Card applications?

Applications are handled by the General Directorate of International Labour Force at the Ministry of Labour and Social Security. Foreigners in Türkiye use the electronic application system; applications from abroad are made through the relevant Turkish diplomatic mission.
