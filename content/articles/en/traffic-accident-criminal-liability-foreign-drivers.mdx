In Turkey — particularly in cities like Antalya with heavy tourist traffic — accidents involving foreign drivers are frequent occurrences. Once a traffic accident occurs, multiple legal fronts open protecting both victim and non-victim parties' rights: criminal, administrative, and civil compensation. For the foreign driver, sound management of these multiple processes is critical to controlling both the criminal file and financial outcomes.

## Consequences of Traffic Accident: Three Fronts

When a traffic accident occurs, three parallel processes typically arise:

### 1. Criminal Investigation

If the accident involves injury or death, the prosecutor's office initiates investigation under offences of **negligent injury** or **negligent causing of death**. While criminal process generally doesn't run for property-only damage:

- Drugs, alcohol, intentional accidents,
- Driving without licence,
- Fleeing the scene

create criminal liability.

### 2. Administrative Process (Traffic Penalties)

Traffic police issue tickets for traffic-rule violations; administrative fines and where appropriate licence withdrawal are applied. For foreign driver:

- Turkish driver licence (if any) may be withdrawn,
- Foreign licence recognition period may be reduced,
- Administrative fines unpaid become subject to enforcement.

### 3. Civil Compensation

Victim's claims for material and moral compensation:

- Met by **traffic insurance** (compulsory financial liability insurance),
- For damages exceeding insurance limits, lawsuit directly against driver or vehicle owner,
- Compensation case heard at civil court.

## Determining Criminal Liability

In Turkish criminal law, traffic accidents are basically evaluated under **negligence**; that is, the person is liable for failing to exercise due care even without intent to commit offence. In determining liability:

- **Expert report** — traffic engineer and physician reports,
- **Scene investigation report**,
- **Alcohol/drug test** (if applicable),
- **Witness statements**,
- **Vehicle damage photos**

are evaluated.

The expert report determines **fault percentages**: parties' contribution to accident is expressed in percentage. You can be qualified as fully at fault, partially at fault, or without fault.

## Driving Under Influence of Alcohol or Drugs

Criminal liability is **aggravated** for drivers under influence of alcohol or drugs. Additionally:

- **Surrender** of driver licence,
- **Vehicle impoundment**,
- Right of **subrogation** arises after traffic insurance pays the damage (the insurer recovers from the at-fault driver)

apply. The misconception of foreign tourists drinking beer or cocktails and then driving in Turkey can have serious consequences; alcohol limit excesses are strictly monitored.

## Fleeing the Scene

Fleeing the scene after accident constitutes a separate offence and **increases** criminal liability. In addition:

- Right of **subrogation** for insurance arises,
- **Driver licence** withdrawn in administrative process,
- For foreigner, **deportation** possibility strengthens.

In Antalya, we've observed for years that foreign tourists panicking and leaving the scene face serious consequences. Stay at the scene; call police and medical teams.

## Insurance Relationship

### Compulsory Traffic Insurance

In Turkey, all motor vehicles must have **compulsory financial liability insurance**. This insurance:

- Covers material and bodily damages of third parties,
- Covers damage to passengers other than driver,
- Pays compensation within specific limits.

### Green Card System for Foreign Drivers

The **Green Card System** applies to drivers coming from European countries to Turkey. Under this:

- Foreign-plate's home-country insurance is recognised in Turkey,
- Compensation processes run through Turkish Insurance Sector Association.

### Comprehensive and Additional Insurance

Comprehensive (kasko) is optional insurance covering damage to driver's own vehicle. If you're using a foreign rental car, you must read the rental contract's **insurance package** carefully — some packages carry significant excess amounts.

## Compensation Lawsuit

When insurance limits are insufficient, the victim may file directly against driver and vehicle owner for:

- **Material compensation** — treatment expenses, loss of earnings, vehicle damage,
- **Moral compensation** — for pain and suffering experienced,
- **Compensation for deprivation of support** — for relatives in case of death.

In setting compensation amount for foreign victims:

- Home-country economic conditions,
- International dimension of medical treatment,
- Translation and travel expenses

may also be considered.

## Protecting Rights as a Foreign Victim

If foreign driver is **victim** in accident:

- Complaint to public prosecutor's office,
- Joining criminal case as **complainant / participant**,
- Damage notification to traffic insurance company,
- Filing compensation case.

Foreign victim's home-country insurance company may also enter; in this case, coordination between two countries' insurance is needed.

## End of Trial: Possible Outcomes

At end of criminal trial, possible outcomes:

- **Acquittal** — for absence of fault or insufficient evidence,
- **Postponement of judgment announcement (HAGB)** — for light offences and first-time offenders,
- **Fine** — common in negligent injury offences,
- **Imprisonment** — particularly in alcohol, fleeing-resulting, severe-result accidents.

When imprisonment is imposed, for foreigner:

- Place sentence is served,
- Possibility of return to home country,
- Conditional release conditions

are evaluated. Foreigners with finalised sentence in Turkey may, under specific conditions, request to serve their sentence in home country.

## Preventive Recommendations for Foreign Drivers

1. **Understand insurance package well before renting in Turkey** — particularly excess amount.
2. **Alcohol and drugs are zero tolerance** — tourist or resident, makes no difference.
3. **Pay attention to speed and traffic rules** — small mistakes can have big consequences in Antalya's busy traffic.
4. **Stay at scene in case of accident** — call medical teams and police.
5. **Call your lawyer immediately** — get legal support before giving statement.
