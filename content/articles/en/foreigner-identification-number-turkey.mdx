Almost every step a foreigner takes in Türkiye — opening a bank account, completing a title deed transaction, establishing a tax liability, receiving healthcare, converting a driving licence — depends on a single number: the foreigner identification number. Yet who issues it, and on what conditions, is frequently misunderstood. The most common misconception is that the number is given only to holders of a residence permit; the law in fact confers a much broader power.

## The Legal Definition and Basis of the Number

Subparagraph (v) of Article 3 of Law No. 6458 on Foreigners and International Protection defines the foreigner identification number as **the identification number issued to foreigners under Law No. 5490 on Population Services of 25/4/2006**. The true source of the number is therefore not migration legislation but civil registration legislation. That distinction matters in practice: issuing the number falls within the competence of the civil registration organisation of the Ministry of Interior, not of the Directorate of Migration Management.

The register in which the number is recorded is also defined by statute. Subparagraph (çç) of Article 3 of Law No. 5490 describes the **foreigners register** as "the register in which the records of foreigners issued with an identification number are kept electronically". It is a register in its own right, separate from the family register of Turkish citizens.

## Who May Obtain One: The Two Routes Created by the Law

Article 8 of Law No. 5490 — amended, together with its heading, by Law No. 7148 of 18/10/2018 — confers two distinct powers on the Ministry.

The first is to issue identification numbers to **foreigners falling within the scope of Law No. 6458** and to record them in the foreigners register. This covers forms of stay governed by migration legislation, such as a residence permit or an international protection application or status.

The second, and less widely known in practice, is this: a foreigner identification number may be issued to **foreigners who apply to a public institution in Türkiye or to our missions abroad in connection with any transaction, where they so request**. This subparagraph shows plainly that the number is not tied to a residence permit. A foreigner who does not reside in Türkiye and merely needs to complete a transaction before a public institution — for an inheritance matter, litigation, or the transfer of immovable property, for example — may obtain a number on request. The possibility of applying at a Turkish consulate abroad arises from the same subparagraph.

The final sentence of the article lays down the sole exception: **members of diplomatic missions fall outside the scope of the provision.**

## The Special Case Where a Number Is Issued Without a Residence Permit

The first paragraph of Article 26 of Law No. 6458 governs a separate situation. Time spent by foreigners held in prison as detainees or convicted persons, or in removal centres under administrative detention, is not treated as a breach of the residence permit period; any residence permits such persons hold may be cancelled. The same paragraph goes on to provide that **those among them who do not hold a foreigner identification number may be issued one without the residence permit condition being sought.**

The provision is a practical safeguard enabling a foreigner whose liberty is restricted to carry out legal acts — granting a power of attorney, bringing proceedings, obtaining healthcare. It may also be decisive for the appointment of a lawyer in the course of an [appeal against a deportation order](/en/articles/deportation-order-appeal-turkey).

## Period of Validity and Documents Required

The second paragraph of Article 8 of Law No. 5490 leaves the detail to secondary legislation: **the period of validity of the foreigner identification number, the documents to be requested at the time of application and thereafter, and other matters are determined by a regulation to be issued by the Ministry of Interior.**

It follows that the list of documents is not fixed by statute. The documents required may be updated by the administration, and the current list should be confirmed before applying. In practice a passport or equivalent document, paperwork establishing the ground for the application, and any residence permit document are typically requested.

That the period of validity is left to regulation also calls for care: it is wrong to think of the number as an open-ended identity document. What becomes of the record when the right of stay underlying the number comes to an end is a matter for administrative regulation.

## Other Documents in Which the Number Appears

The law directly requires certain documents to carry the foreigner identification number:

- **Stateless Person Identity Document**: under Article 50 of Law No. 6458 this document also carries the foreigner identification number. It is subject to no fee whatsoever, takes the place of a residence permit, and is renewed by the governorates every two years.
- **International Protection Applicant Identity Document**: under Article 76, this document — issued to an applicant whose registration has been completed and to any family members who arrived with them — contains the foreigner identification number.
- **Identity document for holders of international protection status**: under Article 83, an identity document containing the foreigner identification number is issued to those granted refugee, conditional refugee, or subsidiary protection status. These documents are subject to no fee whatsoever and take the place of a residence permit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I have no residence permit — can I still obtain a foreigner identification number?

Yes, you can. Article 8 of Law No. 5490 provides that a number may be issued, on request, to foreigners who apply to a public institution in Türkiye or to missions abroad in connection with any transaction. Issuance is not made conditional on holding a residence permit.

### Is the identification number the same thing as a residence permit?

No. The number is an identification number and does not, on its own, confer a right to remain in Türkiye. A residence permit is the right of stay itself. The fact that a person holds a number does not mean that they reside lawfully in Türkiye.

### Can I apply through a consulate?

Yes. Article 8 of Law No. 5490 also covers foreigners applying to missions abroad. Obtaining the number before travelling to Türkiye saves time for [transactions to be carried out under a power of attorney](/en/articles/power-of-attorney-turkey-foreigners).

### Why are members of diplomatic missions excluded?

The law states this expressly. The status of members of diplomatic missions is subject to a separate regime as regards identity and registration; the general regime for foreigner identification numbers does not apply to them.
