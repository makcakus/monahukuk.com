TTK Art. 670-676 open the "Negotiable Order Instruments" chapter of Turkish negotiable instrument law, covering bills of exchange (poliçe) — the model instrument whose rules extend by reference to promissory notes and checks.

## Capacity to Incur a Bill Obligation

Art. 670 ties bill-of-exchange capacity directly to general contractual capacity — no separate regime is created. Turkish courts treat this as a matter of public policy the court must examine on its own motion: a signature by someone lacking legal capacity to discern the consequences of their actions has no legal effect regardless of the counterparty's good faith.

## The Eight Mandatory Elements

Art. 671 requires eight elements: the word "bill of exchange" in the text, an unconditional order to pay a specific sum, the name of the party who must pay (the drawee), the maturity date, the place of payment, the name of the payee, the date and place of issuance, and the drawer's signature. The unconditional payment order is the essence of the instrument — any condition attached to it undermines the negotiability the entire system depends on.

## Consequences of a Missing Element

Under Art. 672, a document missing any of these eight elements is not a bill of exchange — with three narrow exceptions: an unstated maturity date defaults to "payable at sight"; an unstated payment place defaults to the location shown next to the drawee's name; and an unstated place of issuance defaults to the location shown next to the drawer's name. No such fallback exists for the other five elements (the word "bill of exchange," the unconditional order, the drawee's name, the payee's name, and the drawer's signature) — missing any of these is fatal to the instrument's status.

## Special Rules on Identity, Payment Location, Interest, and Conflicting Amounts

A drawer may draw a bill payable to their own order, on themselves, or for a third party's account (Art. 673) — flexibility unique to bills of exchange; Turkish courts have confirmed this does not extend to promissory notes, where the drawer cannot name themselves as payee. Payment may be made through a third party or at a location other than the drawee's residence (Art. 674). An interest clause is only valid on bills payable at sight or at a fixed period after sight — on other bills it is deemed unwritten (Art. 675). Where the amount is stated in both words and figures with a discrepancy, the amount in words prevails; where stated multiple times in the same format with discrepancies, the lowest amount prevails (Art. 676) — though Turkish courts require this rule to yield where the discrepancy is shown to result from forgery rather than a mere clerical error.

## Practical Takeaway

Anyone drafting or accepting a bill of exchange under Turkish law should verify all eight formal elements are present, since a single missing non-curable element (beyond maturity, payment place, or issuance place) strips the document of its status as a bill of exchange entirely.
