For foreign nationals long residing in Turkey, owning property, having established family, **cancellation of residence permit** is a serious legal shock. The cancellation decision affects not only legal residence status but directly children's school, work permit, bank accounts, and entire life arrangement. The judicial route is open against residence permit cancellation in Turkish administrative law; this guide examines the legal dimension of the process.

## Reasons for Residence Permit Cancellation

Migration Administration cancels residence permits for following main reasons:

### 1. Disappearance of Permit Conditions

Residence permit is based on specific reason. When this reason disappears, permit can be cancelled:

- Those obtaining family residence permit and divorcing,
- Leaving job in residence permit tied to work permit,
- Severing relationship with school in student residence permit,
- Disposing of property in permit obtained for real estate ownership.

### 2. Period Exceeded and Non-Renewal

Failure to renew expiring residence permit is automatic cancellation reason.

### 3. Long-Term Absence Abroad

Foreigner resident in Turkey can have permit cancelled when staying abroad for long time. Stays exceeding period limits set in legislation are evaluated in this scope.

### 4. False Declaration or Document

Subsequent emergence of misleading declaration made in application leads to cancellation.

### 5. Criminal Record and Criminal Proceedings

Conviction for offences of specific weight committed by foreigner in Turkey can lead to residence permit cancellation.

### 6. Public Order / Security Reason

Foreigner being assessed as objectionable in terms of public order or security.

### 7. Sham Marriage Detection

Subsequent detection that marriage isn't genuine after family residence permit obtained leads to permit cancellation.

## Notification of Cancellation Decision

Cancellation decision is **notified in writing** to foreigner. Notification:

- Made to address in Turkey,
- If no address declaration, electronic notification or other methods may be used,
- Periods begin to run from notification.

Foreigner must rapidly take action from moment notification is made.

## Judicial Route Against Cancellation Decision

Against residence permit cancellation:

- Within period prescribed by administrative procedure rules,
- At competent **Administrative Court**,
- With **stay of execution** request,

cancellation lawsuit is filed. For transactions made in Antalya, Antalya Administrative Court has jurisdiction.

## Importance of Stay of Execution

When stay of execution isn't requested:

- Foreigner may have to leave country during trial process,
- Deportation processes may be initiated,
- Life in Turkey is disrupted.

When stay of execution decision is granted:

- Foreigner continues to stay in Turkey until trial concludes,
- Consequences from residence permit cancellation decision are temporarily suspended.

Therefore, when filing lawsuit, stay of execution request must be detailedly justified.

## Effective Arguments in Lawsuit

Defence strategy in cancellation lawsuit:

### 1. Emphasising Ties to Turkey

Foreigner's:

- Existence of Turkish-citizen spouse or children,
- Property ownership,
- Business established in Turkey,
- Long-term residence in Turkey,
- Children's status in Turkish education system

forms foundation of defence. **Family unity right** under European Convention on Human Rights framework is strong argument.

### 2. Proportionality Review

In situations where cancellation decision is disproportionate with applicant's behaviour, can be defended that administration used discretion unlawfully. For example, cancellation of permit due to small violation may be disproportionate.

### 3. Insufficiency of Reasoning

Can be defended that cancellation decision has abstract reasoning not based on concrete evidence, not supported by file evidence.

### 4. Equal Treatment Violation

When other foreigners' permits in same conditions are continued while this foreigner's permit is cancelled, can constitute violation of equal treatment principle.

### 5. Formal and Procedural Errors

Formal errors like not granting foreigner right of defence, not allowing file review during cancellation process can affect judgment before judiciary.

## Process Combining with Deportation

Residence permit cancellation decision is often issued along with **deportation order** or deportation order is issued following cancellation. In this case:

- When cancellation lawsuit is filed against deportation order, **its enforcement is automatically suspended**,
- Foreigner cannot be deported until trial concludes,
- A single lawsuit can evaluate both decisions together.

## Risk of Administrative Detention

In some cases, foreigner is placed under **administrative detention** along with residence permit cancellation decision. This situation:

- Placement at Return Centre,
- Magistrates' Court oversight,
- Requires urgent legal intervention.

In administrative detention, application can be made to magistrate to object against detention decision.

## Settlement and Re-Application

In some files, instead of or in parallel with cancellation lawsuit:

- **Re-application for residence permit**,
- **Application for different type of permit** (short-term, family, work)

can be evaluated. Which route to prefer is determined by concrete conditions of file and foreigner's status.

## Post-Decision Process

Positive conclusion of cancellation lawsuit:

- Residence permit is accepted as valid again,
- If restriction code exists, removal is requested,
- Compensation lawsuit possible for losses experienced during trial period (originating from administrative error).

In case of negative conclusion:

- **Appeal** and **cassation** routes are open,
- Application to ECHR is possible in some situations.

## Preventive Approach

Best way for residence permit not to be cancelled is preventive measures:

- Renewal applications made on time,
- Maintaining accuracy of documents and declarations,
- Compliance with rules on long-term stay abroad,
- Documentation of life arrangement in Turkey,
- Avoiding criminal proceedings.
