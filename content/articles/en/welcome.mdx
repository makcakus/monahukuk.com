We are publishing this journal as a practical resource for foreign nationals and international businesses dealing with Turkish law.

## Who this is for

The articles here are written for non-lawyers — typically expats, foreign investors, and executives at companies operating in Turkey. We try to translate Turkish legal concepts into plain English without losing accuracy, and to flag the procedural details that most often catch foreign clients off guard.

## What we will publish

Expect short, focused pieces on:

- **Immigration**: residence permits, citizenship, deportation appeals, work permits
- **Family**: international divorce in Turkey, child custody, alimony, inheritance
- **Real estate**: foreign acquisition rules, title deed disputes, construction defects
- **IT & AI law**: KVKK compliance, cross-border data transfers, AI liability, digital contracts
- **Labor**: severance and notice rights, unfair dismissal, expat employment
- **Commercial**: setting up a Turkish company, contract enforcement, foreign judgments

## A note on legal advice

Nothing on this site is legal advice for your specific situation. Each matter is fact-specific, and Turkish procedural deadlines move quickly — if you think you need help, contact us directly and we will tell you whether and how we can assist.
