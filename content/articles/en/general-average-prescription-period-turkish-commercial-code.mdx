This article closes out the general average chapter of the Turkish Commercial Code (TTK Art. 1272-1285) with its final provision: the prescription period for contribution claims.

## A One-Year Period

Under Art. 1285(1), general average contribution claims prescribe in one year. This places general average among the shorter prescription periods in Turkish maritime law — comparable to the one-year period for cargo loss, damage, or delay claims, and notably shorter than the ten-year period for claims arising from a passenger's death or bodily injury. The short period reflects several practical concerns: general average disputes involve many parties (shipowner, multiple cargo interests, insurers) who all benefit from prompt finality; the underlying calculations are technical and depend on documents and witness evidence that can go stale; and commercial relationships benefit from not being shadowed indefinitely by an old average event.

## When the Clock Starts

Art. 1285(2) ties the start of the period to the ship's arrival at the place determined under Art. 1279 (destination port, or the port where the voyage ends) — the same geographic anchor used to fix where the adjustment itself takes place. This is a deliberate choice: the period does not run from the moment of the casualty itself, but from the objective, verifiable moment the parties, cargo, and documents are actually brought together, which is when a claim can realistically be pursued.

## Practical Takeaway

Turkish courts apply this calculation strictly and literally — in one reported case, a court calculated the one-year period from the vessel's confirmed arrival date at the substitute port (since the ship never reached its contractual destination) and rejected a prescription defense because the lawsuit was filed just within that window. For foreign cargo interests or insurers, this means the "arrival" date — not the date of the casualty — is the critical reference point for calendaring any general average claim in Turkey.

With this provision, the general average chapter of the TTK (Art. 1272-1285) is complete — from the definition of a general average act through contribution debtors, security, the adjustment process, and now prescription.
