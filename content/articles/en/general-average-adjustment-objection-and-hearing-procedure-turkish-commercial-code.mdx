Once the adjuster's report exists, the question becomes how it is reviewed by the court — who can challenge it, on what notice, and by when (TTK Art. 1281).

## Two Options: Approval or Objection

Under Art. 1281(1), interested parties (insurers included) may ask the court at the place designated under Art. 1279 either to approve the adjustment or to object to it — challenging either the classification of the event as general average, or the correctness of the figures. These are two different kinds of challenges: the first is a legal question about whether the four elements of general average (discussed in an earlier article in this series) were actually met; the second accepts the classification but disputes how the numbers were calculated.

## Notice and the Risk of Default Approval

The petition must name the interested parties to be summoned (Art. 1281(2)). The court then requests supporting documents from the adjuster and can order production if needed (Art. 1281(3)). Critically, Art. 1281(4) requires that all interested parties be summoned to a hearing, and the summons must warn them of three things: that supporting documents can be inspected at the court registry, that they may object even before the hearing date, and — most consequentially — that failure to appear on the scheduled day will be treated as consent to the adjustment. The summons must be served at least fifteen days before the hearing, giving parties a meaningful window to prepare.

## Objections Must Be Clear, Specific, and Timely

Art. 1281(5) requires any objection to be raised, at the latest, at the first hearing, and to be stated clearly and in full detail — leaving no room for doubt. For good cause, the judge may grant a single additional period to submit the objection properly, but an objection that is not clearly and fully stated within these windows is treated as if it had never been made.

## Practical Takeaway

For a foreign party with an interest in a Turkish general average adjustment, the strict fifteen-day notice and first-hearing deadline mean that missing the hearing, or raising an objection too vaguely, can be treated as accepting the adjuster's figures outright — early and specific engagement with counsel matters here.
