A German national who owns property in Antalya and falls into dispute with a tenant; a company with Russian shareholders seeking to collect a debt from its Turkish supplier; a foreign manager working in Türkiye who is dismissed — in all three cases the mediation stage must be passed before going to court.

This guide addresses the practical questions that foreign parties encounter in mediation processes in Türkiye.

## Are Foreigners Within Scope? Yes, and Deliberately So

The first article of Law No. 6325 is written so as to leave no doubt: this Law applies to the resolution of private-law disputes arising from matters or transactions of which the parties may freely dispose, **"including those containing a foreign element"**.

The 3rd Civil Chamber of the Court of Cassation confirms the rule: private-law disputes, provided the parties are able to dispose of them freely, remain within the scope of mediation **regardless of whether they contain a foreign element**.

The practical result: the fact that one of the parties is a foreign national, resides abroad, or that the contract is governed by foreign law, does not remove the obligation of mandatory mediation.

## Language: The Most Critical Practical Issue

Mediation sessions are conducted in **Turkish**, and the Law provides no right to a state-funded interpreter. There is no equivalent here of the mandatory right to an interpreter in criminal procedure.

That means the cost of an interpreter falls on the party, and it is an item of "expense" separate from the mediation fee.

There are two solutions:

**Attending with an interpreter.** A sworn translator should be preferred so that legal terms requiring expertise are conveyed accurately. The interpreter, as a person attending the negotiations, is also bound by confidentiality.

**Attending with bilingual counsel.** This is the safest route in practice. It is possible to attend the negotiations through a lawyer, and the lawyer's signature makes the settlement document valid; moreover, a document signed jointly by the parties and their lawyers together with the mediator is deemed a document having the character of a judgment without an annotation of enforceability.

The importance of language emerges after signature: proving a later allegation of "defective consent" in respect of a document signed without fully understanding its content is extremely difficult. The Court of Cassation regards an allegation of fraud as unproven where a party who admits having signed the information minutes and the settlement document fails to support it with concrete evidence.

## Power of Attorney and Apostille

A party located abroad must execute a power of attorney to be represented by a lawyer in the mediation process in Türkiye. There are two routes:

**Execution at a Turkish consulate.** A power of attorney executed at the Republic of Türkiye's consulate in your country is directly valid; no apostille is additionally required.

**Execution before a foreign notary + apostille.** A power of attorney executed before a notary in a country party to the Hague Apostille Convention must bear an apostille and be accompanied by a sworn Turkish translation. In countries not party to the Convention, consular certification is required.

The scope of the power of attorney is critical: if a **settlement is to be signed** in the mediation, the power must expressly include the authority to settle, to grant a release and to waive. A general litigation power of attorney may not cover those powers, and insufficient authority renders the validity of the signed settlement debatable.

## Do You Have to Come to Türkiye for the Session?

No — and there are three ways.

**Representation by counsel.** Attending the negotiations through a lawyer suffices. However, under a rule added in 2023, the mediator must inform **you as well, even where you have a lawyer**; providing correct contact details is therefore important.

**Online attendance.** The parties may freely agree the mediation procedure, provided it does not conflict with mandatory rules of law. Conducting the session by video conference falls within that, and is widely used for parties located abroad.

**Notifying an excuse.** If you are unable to attend, notify the mediator of your excuse in advance. The mediator may move the meeting to another date within the statutory period.

The cost of non-attendance is real: a party who fails without valid excuse to attend the first meeting is liable, even if successful in the action to be brought, for half of the other side's litigation costs and half of the attorney's fee determined under the Minimum Attorney's Fee Tariff.

## The Most Frequent Disputes for Foreigners

**Leases and real estate.** The busiest field for foreigners owning property in Antalya. Disputes arising from a lease relationship have been within mandatory mediation since 1 September 2023. The competent bureau is at the place where the property is located.

**Employee claims.** A foreign employee employed in Türkiye with a work permit enjoys the same protections as a Turkish employee, and employee claims fall within mandatory mediation.

**Commercial receivables.** Claims of companies with foreign shareholders for payment, damages, removal of objection, negative declaration and restitution fall within Article 5/A TCC; the period is six weeks.

**Dissolution of joint ownership and succession.** Disputes over division among foreign heirs holding immovable property in Türkiye fall within Article 18/B.

**Transfer of immovable property.** Disputes concerning the transfer of immovable property or the creation of a limited right in rem over it are amenable to mediation; but the settlement document must be drawn up having regard to the **restrictions on the acquisition of immovable property by foreigners**. In the annotation-of-enforceability examination the court also reviews compliance with the statutory restrictions concerning immovable property, and may request information and documents from institutions and organisations.

## Enforcement of the Settlement Abroad

Enforcing in another country a settlement reached in a mediation conducted in Türkiye is subject to a separate regime.

Türkiye signed the United Nations Convention on International Settlement Agreements Resulting from Mediation, known as the **Singapore Convention**, on 7 August 2019; its approval was found appropriate by Law No. 7282, and the Convention entered into force for Türkiye on **11 April 2022**.

The Convention covers only settlement agreements that are **commercial** and **international**; agreements arising from consumer transactions and agreements relating to family, succession and employment law are outside its scope.

In the opposite direction — enforcing in Türkiye a mediation settlement made abroad — Article 17/A of Law No. 6325 applies: for the performance of settlement documents within the scope of the Convention, it is compulsory to obtain the annotation of enforceability from the **commercial court of first instance**. Jurisdiction is tiered: the place agreed by the parties, the other party's domicile in Türkiye, their place of residence, and finally one of the courts of Ankara, Istanbul or Izmir.

## The Possibility of Legal Aid

A party unable to meet the mediation fee — being a foreign national does not affect this right — may benefit from legal aid by decision of the civil court of peace of the place where the mediation bureau is located; Articles 334 to 340 of the Code of Civil Procedure apply by analogy.

In addition, in mandatory mediation where no settlement is reached and the session did not exceed two hours, the two-hour fee is paid from the budget of the Ministry of Justice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: I do not speak Turkish — how is the session conducted?

Sessions are conducted in Turkish and there is no right to a state-provided interpreter. The cost of an interpreter or bilingual counsel is yours. In practice the safest route is to attend with a bilingual lawyer.

### Q: I do not live in Türkiye — how do I take part in the process?

You may take part through your lawyer or by video conference. Since the parties may freely agree the mediation procedure, your request for an online session should be conveyed to the mediator and the other party.

### Q: My power of attorney was executed abroad — is it valid?

If executed at a Turkish consulate, it is directly valid. If executed before a foreign notary, an apostille and a sworn Turkish translation are required. If a settlement is to be signed, the power must include the authority to settle, to grant a release and to waive.

### Q: In what language is the settlement document drawn up?

The document is drawn up in Turkish. Having it translated so that its content is understood is advisable; but the legally binding text is the Turkish one. It is therefore critical that the text be fully understood before signature.

### Q: Will a mediation settlement made in Türkiye be valid in my country?

For commercial and international settlement agreements, the Singapore Convention mechanism may operate; but for that, both Türkiye and the country in which enforcement is sought must be party to the Convention, and the settlement must fall within its scope. Agreements relating to consumer, family, succession and employment law are outside it.
