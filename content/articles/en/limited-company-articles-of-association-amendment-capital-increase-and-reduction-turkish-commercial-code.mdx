Once formed, a Turkish limited company's articles of association are not frozen. TTK Art. 589-592 govern how they can change — most often through capital increases and reductions.

## The General Amendment Rule

Art. 589 sets the default threshold for amending the articles at partners representing two-thirds of capital, unless the articles specify otherwise. Certain especially significant changes (altering the business purpose, restricting share transfers, limiting pre-emption rights, relocating abroad) require an even higher bar under Art. 621: two-thirds of votes represented plus an absolute majority of all capital carrying voting rights. Every amendment must be registered and published — an unregistered resolution has no effect on third parties and, Turkish courts have held, a claim to annul an unregistered capital increase resolution lacks legal interest since it never took legal effect in the first place.

## Capital Increases Follow Incorporation Rules

Art. 590 does not create separate increase procedures; it simply applies the incorporation rules — particularly around in-kind contributions and business acquisitions — treating a capital increase as, functionally, a small re-incorporation with the same valuation safeguards.

## Pre-Emption Rights Protect Against Dilution

Art. 591 gives every partner the right to participate in a capital increase proportional to their existing share, protecting against dilution. This right can only be restricted or eliminated for just cause and with the heightened Art. 621(e) majority — examples of just cause include acquiring a business or bringing employees into the company — and even then, no one may be unfairly favored or disadvantaged. Partners get at least fifteen days to exercise the right. Courts have annulled capital increase resolutions where a minority partner's stake was diluted from 30% to 1% without properly affording this right.

## Capital Reduction Requires Clearing Additional Payment Obligations First

Art. 592 applies joint-stock company capital reduction rules by analogy, including creditor notice and protection. Critically, where reduction aims to fix an over-indebted balance sheet, it can only proceed once any additional payment obligations set out in the articles have been fully paid — preventing partners from using a capital reduction to sidestep an obligation designed to protect creditors.

## Practical Takeaway

Investors joining a Turkish limited company should confirm pre-emption rights were properly honored in any past capital increase — a dilution executed without following Art. 591's procedure and majority requirements is vulnerable to annulment.
